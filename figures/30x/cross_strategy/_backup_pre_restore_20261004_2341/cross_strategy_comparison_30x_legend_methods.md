## Figure legend

**Figure 5. Alignment- and assembly-based long-read analysis of GIAB samples against GRCh38.**
Read alignment (left; minimap2, pbmm2, VACmap, VG Giraffe) and de novo assembly (right; Flye,
GoldRush, Verkko) of HG002, HG003 and HG004 at ~30× ONT and PacBio HiFi coverage are shown in
separate columns. Each point is one GIAB sample (colour). The samples are individual biological
observations, not technical replicates. Marker shape and the bracket under each axis give the
sequencing input. Black bars and grey lines give the mean ± SD of the three samples (n = 3). They
are descriptive only, and no statistical test was made between strategies. All y-axes are linear,
start at zero and are set independently for each subpanel, because no row pairs two numerically
identical quantities.
**(a)** Sequence disagreement metrics. Reads: NM edit distance per 100 kbp of CIGAR-mapped read
bases (M/=/X/I). NM counts substituted, inserted and deleted bases, so it includes indel bases.
Contigs: QUAST mismatches (substitutions only) per 100 kbp of aligned contig bases. The two
metrics describe related but not identical forms of disagreement.
**(b)** Indel disagreement, counted as events, not bases. Reads: CIGAR insertion plus deletion
operations of 1–300 bp per 100 kbp of CIGAR-mapped read bases. Contigs: QUAST indel events per
100 kbp of aligned contig bases.
**(c)** Reference representation, a conceptual comparison of two metrics with different
denominators. Reads: CIGAR-aligned yield, the percentage of input sequencing bases represented by
CIGAR alignment. Contigs: genome fraction, the percentage of GRCh38 covered by aligned assembly
sequence. The two percentages are not numerically equivalent and are not compared directly.
Flye and GoldRush are collapsed haploid assemblies. Verkko (ONT+HiFi; shaded) is a diploid
assembly that contains sequence from both haplotypes (QUAST duplication ratio 2.04–2.09). It is
shown separately because its values, especially genome fraction, are not directly equivalent to
those of collapsed assemblies.

## Methods (metric definitions and normalization)

Alignment metrics were taken from `samtools stats` (no -F/-f/-q/-d filters: secondary alignments
excluded, primary and supplementary alignments included, no mapping-quality filter). Assembly
metrics were taken from QUAST v5.3.0 (`--large --min-contig 500`) run against the same GRCh38
GIAB analysis-set reference.

Sequence disagreement for alignments was calculated as samtools `mismatches` divided by
CIGAR-mapped bases × 10⁵. samtools derives `mismatches` from the NM tag, so the value is an edit
distance that includes inserted and deleted bases. The source tables contain no
substitution-only read metric. Subtracting the 1–300 bp indel bases from NM does not give one,
because CIGAR insertions and deletions longer than 300 bp fall outside the samtools indel
histogram and would remain in the difference. QUAST "# mismatches per 100 kbp" counts
substitutions only, normalized to the total aligned contig length.

Indel disagreement for alignments was the number of CIGAR insertion and deletion operations,
summed from the samtools "ID" records, divided by CIGAR-mapped bases × 10⁵. These records cover
indels of 1–300 bp only. For assemblies the metric was QUAST "# indels per 100 kbp", which counts
events, with consecutive indel positions counted once. QUAST counts indels only within
alignments, and gaps longer than 200 bp are classified as local misassemblies. Read-level rates
reflect per-read sequencing and alignment error at ~30× redundancy, whereas contig-level rates
reflect consensus error.

CIGAR-aligned yield was calculated as CIGAR-mapped bases divided by the input sequencing bases of
each sample and technology. The input was taken as the largest samtools `total length` among the
four aligners. Genome fraction is the QUAST percentage of GRCh38 bases covered by aligned contigs.

Verkko was run with `--hifi` and `--nano` only, without trio or Hi-C phasing input. Its
`assembly.fasta` therefore contains both haplotypes, separated locally within graph bubbles but
not phased into two chromosome-scale haplotype sets. Duplication ratio and absolute misassembly
counts are not comparable with collapsed assemblies and are not shown.

HG002, HG003 and HG004 were treated as three biological samples per configuration (n = 3).
SD uses ddof = 1. Missing values would be left blank and marked n/a. None occurred.
