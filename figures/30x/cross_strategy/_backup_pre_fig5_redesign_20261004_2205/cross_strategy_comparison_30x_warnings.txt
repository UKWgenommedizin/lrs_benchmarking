Panel A: aligner and assembler 'mismatches' are NOT the same quantity. samtools stats 'mismatches' is the summed NM tag (edit distance = substitutions + inserted + deleted bases) per CIGAR-mapped read base; QUAST '# mismatches per 100 kbp' counts substitutions only per aligned contig base. Plotted as separate subpanels with independent y-axes; not merged, not tested.
3. Panel B: both sides are indel EVENT counts (not indel bases) per 100 kbp of aligned query bases. The alignment numerator is the sum of CIGAR I/D operations from samtools stats ID records, which only histogram indels of 1-300 bp; longer CIGAR I/D operations are not counted (their inserted bases still enter bases_mapped_cigar and NM). Each subpanel has its own y-axis because the query differs (individual raw reads, each locus counted ~30x, vs consensus contigs) and QUAST only counts indels inside alignments >=95% identity (indels >200 bp become local misassemblies). No statistical comparison is made across strategies.

They are not combined into one score and not compared statistically.
