## Figure legend

**Figure 5. Cross-strategy comparison of alignment- and assembly-based long-read genome analysis.**
GIAB HG002, HG003 and HG004 were analysed at ~30× with ONT and PacBio HiFi reads. Left: read alignment with minimap2, pbmm2, VACmap and VG Giraffe. Right: de novo assembly with Flye and GoldRush (collapsed haploid) and Verkko (ONT+HiFi, haplotype-resolved; hatched background).
Each point is one sample. Color indicates the sample and marker shape the sequencing input. Grey bars show the mean ± SD across the three biological samples and are descriptive only.
**(a)** Base-level disagreement with GRCh38. For alignments, NM-tag edit distance (substitutions plus indel bases) per 100 kbp of CIGAR-mapped read bases. For assemblies, QUAST substitutions per 100 kbp of aligned contig bases. Each subpanel has its own y-axis because the two quantities are defined differently.
**(b)** Indel events per 100 kbp of aligned query bases: CIGAR insertion plus deletion operations for reads, and QUAST indel events for contigs. Both subpanels share a y-scale.
**(c)** Reference representation. For alignments, CIGAR-aligned yield as a percentage of input sequencing bases. For assemblies, QUAST genome fraction as a percentage of GRCh38. These are distinct metrics and are not compared directly.
All axes are linear and start at zero. No statistical tests were performed between strategies.

## Methods (metric definitions and normalization)

Alignment metrics were taken from `samtools stats` on each 30× alignment. Assembly metrics were taken from QUAST v5.3.0 (`--large --min-contig 500`) run against the same GRCh38 GIAB analysis-set reference.

Base-level disagreement for alignments was calculated as samtools `mismatches` divided by CIGAR-mapped bases × 10⁵. Because samtools derives `mismatches` from the NM tag, this value is an edit distance that includes inserted and deleted bases. QUAST "# mismatches per 100 kbp" counts substitutions only, normalized to the total aligned contig length. The two were therefore plotted separately.

Indel disagreement for alignments was the number of CIGAR insertion and deletion operations, taken from samtools "ID" records, divided by CIGAR-mapped bases × 10⁵. For assemblies it was QUAST "# indels per 100 kbp", which is also an event count rather than a base count. QUAST counts only indels inside alignments of ≥95% identity, and gaps longer than 200 bp are classified as local misassemblies. Read-level rates reflect per-read sequencing and alignment error at ~30× redundancy, whereas contig-level rates reflect consensus error, so the two rates were not tested against each other.

CIGAR-aligned yield was calculated as CIGAR-mapped bases divided by the input sequencing bases of each sample and technology. VACmap is normalized to the same input as the other aligners, even though it reports fewer input bases. Genome fraction is the QUAST percentage of GRCh38 bases covered by aligned contigs. Verkko assemblies contain both haplotypes, so their duplication ratio and absolute misassembly count are not comparable to collapsed assemblies and are not shown.

Computational cost would be reported as allocated thread-hours (wall-clock hours × allocated threads) for both strategies. Measured CPU time was unavailable for VACmap and VG Giraffe. Panel D was not drawn because assembler resource measurements were not yet available.

HG002, HG003 and HG004 were treated as three biological samples per configuration (n = 3), not as technical replicates. Missing values were left blank and were never estimated or imputed.
